Your goal

Two goals. One safe plan.

Lose fat or build muscle. Choose a pace and Morsel sets your calories and protein, and it won't let you go faster than is safe.

Lose fat Never faster than 0.75% of your body weight a week.

Build muscle A steady surplus to build on.

Protein is set at 1.8 g per kg of your body weight and updates every time you weigh in. Calories never drop below a safe minimum.

Workouts

Plan five workouts. Do three. Morsel notices.

Your plan's workouts count toward your food target from day one. After two weeks, Morsel stops trusting the plan, counts the workouts you actually finished, and moves your target to match. No more eating for the gym session you skipped.

  • The timer keeps running even if your phone closes the app

Forecast

A finish date based on you, not a formula.

At first, Morsel's forecast uses your plan's numbers. Once you've fully logged 7 days, it switches to your real pace. Days you only half logged don't count, so they can't skew the forecast.

  • Milestone dates for losing 5% and 10% of your weight
  • A curve that slows over time, like real weight loss does
  • Badges for logging streaks, step records and lift PRs
