Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

Morsel is a nutrition and fitness app. This policy explains what data Morsel keeps, where it is kept, and the few times anything leaves your phone.

Data stored on your device

Morsel stores your profile, goals, meals, weight, activity summaries, progress photos, preferences, and private product-usage counters in an on-device database. Morsel does not require an account and does not send this data to a Morsel server. There is no Morsel server holding your history.

Health access

If you choose to connect Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS), Morsel asks to read only these categories: steps, distance, exercise sessions, active and total calories burned, and heart rate. You can approve or refuse each one. Morsel does not write anything to Health Connect or Apple Health.

Morsel stores summaries of this activity on your device so it can show them in your day and your progress. Health data is never used for advertising and is never sold. You can disconnect health access in Morsel's Settings or in your phone's system settings.

Camera and photos

Morsel uses the camera to scan food barcodes and, if you choose, to take progress photos. Progress photos you take or pick from your gallery are kept inside the app on your device.

Barcode lookup

When you scan a packaged-food barcode, Morsel sends that barcode number to Open Food Facts to look up public product information. Your profile and meal history are not included in that request.

Exercise animations

The first time you open an exercise, Morsel downloads its animation from GitHub and keeps a copy on your device. Like any web request, this shares your IP address with GitHub. No information about you or your workouts is sent. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Subscriptions

Morsel is a paid subscription. Purchases are handled by Google Play (or the App Store on iPhone). Morsel never sees your card or payment details.

Morsel uses RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device and app information such as platform, app version and store country. It does not receive your name, email, meals, weight or health data.

No ads, no analytics, no selling

Morsel shows no ads and contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Morsel does not sell or share your personal data.

Notifications and exports

Meal and activity reminders are scheduled on your device. Your data leaves the app only when you choose to create a backup or feedback export and share that file with an app or person you select.

Your controls and deletion

  • Reset your logged data from Morsel's Settings.
  • Revoke camera, notification or health permissions at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Remove all of Morsel's data from your phone by uninstalling the app.

Because Morsel has no account and no Morsel cloud database, there is no remote account record to delete. To manage or cancel your subscription, use Google Play (or the App Store on iPhone).

Health estimates

Calorie, macro and activity numbers in Morsel are estimates to guide you. They are not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before making major changes to your diet or exercise.

Changes to this policy
